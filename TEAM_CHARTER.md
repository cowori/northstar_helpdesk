Team Working Agreement & Charter

- Primary Channel: [WhatsApp / Slack / Discord] for daily syncs.
- Response Time: Acknowledge messages within 4 hours during working hours.
- Updates: Post progress updates at the end of each workday.

- Code commits must be pushed to individual feature branches before merging.

- Disagreements will first be discussed between involved parties.
